### 2026년 3월 공장별 탄소 배출량은 얼마야?
(34.3초)
답: This query calculates total production quantity per factory for March 2026 by joining production lots (lot) through work orders (wo), production lines (line), and plant identifiers (plant). It filters production dates to the calendar month of March 2026 and groups the results by plant code and name, summing the per-lot production quantities. Note that carbon emission data is not available in the schema; total_produced_qty serves as the best available proxy for factory-level activity during the specified period.
SQL:
SELECT p.plant_cd, p.plant_nm, SUM(l.prod_qty) AS total_produced_qty FROM factory.mes.lot AS l JOIN factory.mes.wo AS w ON l.wo_no = w.wo_no JOIN factory.mes.line AS ln ON w.line_cd = ln.line_cd JOIN factory.mes.plant AS p ON ln.plant_cd = p.plant_cd WHERE l.prod_dt BETWEEN '2026-03-01' AND '2026-03-31' GROUP BY p.plant_cd, p.plant_nm ORDER BY p.plant_cd;
