(77.0초)
답: This query calculates total production quantity per factory (plant) for March 2026. It starts from the production lot table and joins through work order, production line, and plant to attribute each lot to a plant code. The prod_dt filter restricts to March 2026 records, and the sum of prod_qty aggregates production volume per plant. Results are grouped by plant code and ordered alphabetically.
