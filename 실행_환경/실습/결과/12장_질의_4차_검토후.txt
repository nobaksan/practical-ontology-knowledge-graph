### 공장별 검사 불량률을 알려줘
(16.4초)
답: This query calculates the inspection defect rate per factory by joining inspection records (qms.insp) through the production path (lot → wo → line) to the plant table. It sums the defective units (dfct_qty) and sampled units (smpl_qty) for each factory, casts the totals to REAL before dividing to preserve fractional precision, and groups the results by plant code and name, ordering by plant code.
SQL:
SELECT p.plant_cd, p.plant_nm, CAST(SUM(i.dfct_qty) AS REAL) / CAST(SUM(i.smpl_qty) AS REAL) AS defect_rate FROM factory.qms.insp i JOIN factory.mes.lot l ON l.lot_no = i.lot_no JOIN factory.mes.wo w ON w.wo_no = l.wo_no JOIN factory.mes.line ln ON ln.line_cd = w.line_cd JOIN factory.mes.plant p ON p.plant_cd = ln.plant_cd GROUP BY p.plant_cd, p.plant_nm ORDER BY p.plant_cd;
